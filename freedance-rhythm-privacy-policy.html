Privacy Policy for FreeDance Rhythm

Developer: Tan Bkiat
Last updated: October 26, 2026

FreeDance Rhythm is a rhythm game that does not require a personal account. Most game settings and progress are stored locally on the user's device. Online leaderboard features use pseudonymous player and gameplay information as described below.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how FreeDance Rhythm ("the app", "we", "us", or "our") accesses, processes, stores, and shares information when you use the game. By using FreeDance Rhythm, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

2. Information Processed by the App

FreeDance Rhythm may process the following categories of information:

  • Player identifier and display name: a pseudonymous identifier and an automatically generated or player-selected display name used for leaderboard entries.
  • Gameplay and leaderboard data: scores, ranks, selected songs, difficulty, accuracy, combo, performance results, and related timestamps needed to operate and protect the online leaderboard.
  • Local game data: settings, preferences, progress, unlocked content, locally generated note charts, and other game state stored on the device.
  • Technical request information: standard network information such as an IP address, app instance information, and security or request metadata may be processed by our service provider when the app communicates with online services.

We do not ask for your legal name, postal address, phone number, payment card details, or precise location to use the game's core features.

3. Online Leaderboards

FreeDance Rhythm provides an online leaderboard for eligible built-in songs. When you submit a result, the app may send your pseudonymous player identifier, display name, score, ranking criteria, song or chart identifier, selected difficulty, performance statistics, and a submission timestamp to the leaderboard service.

Leaderboard information is visible to other players. You should not use your real name, email address, phone number, or other sensitive information as your public display name. Results from songs imported by the user are not intended to be submitted to the official leaderboard.

4. User-Imported Music and Files

The app may allow you to select compatible audio files from your device and generate playable note charts. File selection is initiated by you through the device's file picker. Imported audio files and locally generated charts are processed and stored on your device and are not uploaded by FreeDance Rhythm to the official leaderboard or the developer's server.

You are responsible for ensuring that you have permission to use any music or file you import. Deleting an imported song inside the app, clearing the app's data, or uninstalling the app may remove associated local data.

5. Firebase and Other Service Providers

FreeDance Rhythm uses Google Firebase services, including Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions, to operate the online leaderboard, validate submissions, reduce abuse, and deliver related online features. Google may process information on our behalf according to its own terms and privacy practices.

The app may also use Google Play services for app distribution, updates, integrity, security, and purchase processing where applicable. These providers may independently process technical information under their own privacy policies.

More information about Google's privacy practices is available at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

6. How We Use Information

Information is used only as reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide gameplay, local progress, settings, and imported-song features
  • Submit, calculate, display, and maintain leaderboard rankings
  • Validate results and prevent cheating, fraud, spam, or abuse
  • Maintain the security, reliability, and performance of online services
  • Respond to privacy, support, and data-deletion requests
  • Comply with applicable law and enforce relevant terms

7. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell personal or sensitive user data.

Information may be disclosed to Google Firebase and other service providers only as needed to operate the app and its online features. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users, or to investigate security and abuse. Public leaderboard entries are shared with other players as part of the feature.

8. Advertising

FreeDance Rhythm does not currently display third-party advertising and does not use leaderboard information for targeted advertising. If this changes, this Privacy Policy and the app's Google Play Data safety disclosures will be updated before the change is released.

9. Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you delete it through the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Leaderboard data may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide rankings, preserve competition integrity, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations.

You may request deletion of your leaderboard data by emailing albertloh2577@gmail.com. Include your in-game player identifier and display name so that we can locate the relevant pseudonymous record. We may need to verify that the request relates to your game installation. Deletion from active systems will be completed within a reasonable period, subject to security, fraud prevention, backup, and legal-retention requirements.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information, including Firebase security controls and server-side validation for online submissions. However, no electronic storage or network transmission method is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. International Processing

Online service providers may process and store information in countries other than your own. Privacy and data-protection laws in those locations may differ from those in your country.

12. Children's Privacy

FreeDance Rhythm is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly request or collect a child's legal name, contact details, or precise location. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us so that we can review and, where appropriate, delete it.

13. Your Choices

You may:

  • Avoid entering personal information in your public display name
  • Use the app without importing personal audio files
  • Use offline gameplay features without submitting eligible scores when available
  • Delete locally stored game data through the app or Android settings
  • Request deletion of leaderboard data using the contact details below

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when features, data practices, legal requirements, or third-party services change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated date.
