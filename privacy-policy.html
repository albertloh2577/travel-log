Privacy Policy for Gig Profit Tracker

Developer: Tan Bkiat
Last updated: July 28, 2026

Gig Profit Tracker is an offline-first business, profit, expense, and mileage tracking app. The app does not require an account. Trip records, location history, income, expenses, and other business records are stored locally on the user's device unless the user chooses to export, back up, or share them.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Gig Profit Tracker accesses, processes, stores, and handles information when you use the app. Gig Profit Tracker is designed for gig workers, delivery riders, ride-hailing drivers, and other users who want to record business trips, mileage, income, fuel costs, tolls, parking, meals, maintenance, vehicle details, driving areas, and related reports.

2. Information Processed by the App

Gig Profit Tracker processes information needed to provide its features. Most information is entered by the user or generated by the app and is stored locally on the user's device.

The app may process the following information:

  • Trip start and stop times, duration, distance, and route information
  • Precise GPS location points used for trip recording, route history, mileage calculation, and driving-area features
  • Physical activity status, such as whether the device appears to be in a vehicle, for Auto Trip Detection
  • Income and business records entered by the user
  • Fuel, toll, parking, meal, maintenance, loan, insurance, and other expense records entered by the user
  • Vehicle information entered by the user
  • App settings and permission status
  • PDF reports and backup files generated by the user

3. Location Data

Gig Profit Tracker uses precise location data to:

  • Automatically detect and record business trips
  • Record driving routes
  • Calculate mileage and trip distance
  • Display route history and driving-area information
  • Generate accurate trip records and reports

Location permission is required for core trip-recording functionality. When background location is enabled, Gig Profit Tracker may access location while the app is running in the background, when the app is not visible, or when the screen is off so ongoing trips can continue to be recorded and Auto Trip Detection can operate.

Location and trip records are stored locally on your device. Gig Profit Tracker does not operate its own server for uploading or storing your location history, and the developer does not sell your location data.

You can disable or change location permission at any time through your device settings. Disabling location access may prevent trip recording, route history, mileage calculation, or Auto Trip Detection from working correctly.

4. Physical Activity Data

Gig Profit Tracker may request Physical Activity permission to help determine whether the device appears to be travelling in a vehicle. This information supports Auto Trip Detection and may help reduce unnecessary GPS use when the user is not driving.

Physical activity information is processed on the device for app functionality and is not uploaded to the developer's own server.

5. Local Storage

Gig Profit Tracker stores app records locally on your device. This may include trip history, GPS route points, income, expenses, vehicle information, reports, settings, and other records created through the app.

If you uninstall the app, clear its app data, reset the device, or lose access to the device, locally stored records may be lost unless you created and retained a backup.

6. Backup and Restore

Gig Profit Tracker may allow you to create backup files and restore records from backup files. Backup and restore actions are initiated by the user.

If you save, upload, or share a backup through cloud storage, email, messaging, a file manager, or another third-party service, the transfer is initiated and controlled by you. The selected third-party service may process the file under its own privacy policy and terms.

7. PDF Export and Sharing

Gig Profit Tracker may generate PDF reports on your device. Reports may contain trip, income, expense, vehicle, mileage, maintenance, or driving-area information selected by the user.

If you save or share a PDF through another app or service, that action is initiated by you and may be subject to the privacy practices of the selected service.

8. Maps and Third-Party Services

Gig Profit Tracker may use map tiles, open map data, Android system services, and device APIs to provide maps, location permissions, activity recognition, notifications, file access, sharing, and related functionality.

When map content is loaded, the relevant map or network provider may receive standard technical request information, such as an IP address, device or browser information, and the requested map tile. Such processing is governed by the provider's own privacy policy.

Gig Profit Tracker does not use location data for advertising and does not sell personal or sensitive user data.

9. Accounts and Login

Gig Profit Tracker does not require users to create an account or sign in to use its core features.

10. Payments and Subscriptions

If Gig Profit Tracker offers in-app purchases or subscriptions, payments are processed by Google Play. The developer does not receive or store your full payment card details. Payment processing is subject to Google Play's terms and privacy practices.

11. Data Sharing and Sale

The developer does not:

  • Sell your personal or sensitive user data
  • Sell your location history
  • Upload your trip records or GPS history to the developer's own server
  • Use your location data for advertising

Data may leave your device only when necessary to load a third-party service such as map content, when processed by Google Play for purchases, or when you intentionally export, back up, or share a file using another app or service.

12. Children's Privacy

Gig Profit Tracker is intended for drivers, riders, gig workers, and business record-keeping. It is not directed to children, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through an account or developer-operated server.

13. Data Security

Gig Profit Tracker uses an offline-first design to reduce unnecessary data transfer. However, no device or method of electronic storage is completely secure. Users are responsible for protecting their device, screen lock, backup files, exported reports, and any third-party account used to store or share files.

14. User Choices and Control

You can control your information by:

  • Editing or deleting records inside the app
  • Enabling, disabling, or changing location permission in Android settings
  • Enabling or disabling Auto Trip Detection
  • Managing Physical Activity and notification permissions in Android settings
  • Creating, retaining, sharing, or deleting backup and PDF files
  • Clearing app data or uninstalling the app

15. Data Retention and Deletion

Records remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app's data, uninstall the app, or the device storage is otherwise erased. Backup files and exported reports remain wherever you saved them until you delete them from that location.

Because Gig Profit Tracker does not require an account and does not store app records on the developer's own server, there is no server-side user account or cloud record for the developer to delete.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features, data practices, legal requirements, or third-party services change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
