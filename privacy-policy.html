Privacy Policy for Travel Log

Last updated: July 4, 2026

Travel Log is designed as an offline-first driver business and mileage tracking app. The app does not require an account and does not upload your trip records to our own server.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Travel Log handles information when you use the app. Travel Log is a driver business management and mileage tracking application designed to help users record trips, income, petrol/fuel expenses, tolls, meals, maintenance expenses, vehicle records, driving areas, and related reports.

2. Information We Collect

Travel Log does not collect personal information to our own server. The app stores user-entered records locally on the user's device.

The app may process the following information on your device:

  • Trip start and stop time
  • GPS location points used to calculate distance and display route history
  • Income records entered by the user
  • Petrol/fuel, toll, parking, meal, and maintenance records entered by the user
  • Vehicle information entered by the user
  • PDF reports and backup files generated by the user

3. Location Data

Travel Log uses device location permission to record trips, calculate distance, show routes on the map, and support features such as trip history, driving area analytics, and auto trip detection.

Location data is processed and stored locally on your device. Travel Log does not upload your location history to our own server.

If background location is enabled, the app may use location access while the app is running in the background for trip tracking and auto trip detection. You can control location permission at any time through your device settings.

4. Local Storage

Travel Log stores records on your own device using local app storage. This includes trip history, route data, expense records, income records, vehicle information, settings, and other app records.

If you delete the app or clear app data, locally stored records may be deleted unless you have created a backup.

5. Backup and Restore

Travel Log may allow you to create backup files and restore records from backup files. Backup and restore actions are initiated by the user.

If you share, save, or upload a backup file to another service such as cloud storage, messaging apps, email, or file managers, that action is controlled by you and may be subject to the privacy policy of the selected third-party service.

6. PDF Export and Sharing

Travel Log may allow you to generate PDF reports for trips, income, petrol/fuel, maintenance, and driving area data. PDF files are generated on your device. If you choose to share or save a PDF using another app or service, that action is controlled by you.

7. Third-Party Services

Travel Log may use map display services or open map data to display maps and routes. The app may also use standard Android system services for permissions, file sharing, and device storage.

Travel Log does not sell your personal data.

8. Accounts and Login

Travel Log does not require users to create an account or sign in to use the core app features.

9. Payments and Subscriptions

If Travel Log offers in-app purchases or subscriptions, payments are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your full payment card details. Payment handling is subject to Google Play's terms and privacy policies.

10. Children's Privacy

Travel Log is intended for drivers and business record-keeping use. It is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Data Security

We use an offline-first approach to reduce unnecessary data transfer. However, no method of electronic storage is completely secure. Users are responsible for keeping their device, backup files, and exported reports safe.

12. User Control

You can control your data by:

  • Editing or deleting records inside the app
  • Managing location permission in Android settings
  • Creating backup files when needed
  • Deleting the app or clearing app data from your device settings

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
